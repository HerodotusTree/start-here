# The Grove — Start Here

**Own the thing.**

A node is a small computer in your home that works for your house — and only your house. It holds your family's photos, files, and records on ground you own. It runs a resident AI that lives there, learns your household's way of doing things, and answers to you.

No landlord. No bundle. No door that somebody else can close.

## Why this exists

In November 2026, Google announced that schools which opted out of its AI tools would also lose Google Search for their students. After a day of public refusal, Google reversed itself. The lesson outlived the headline:

**A road that can be closed on you was never your road.**

Every tool you rent carries a landlord. The Grove is the alternative being built in the open: local-first machines, owned outright, running software anyone can read.

## What a node is

- **Yours.** Your hardware, your house, your rules. There is no remote switch, no admin backdoor, no tether — by design, not by policy.
- **Local-first.** Your data lives on your machine. The network is for reaching other people's nodes when *you* choose to, not for reporting home.
- **Resident AI.** Each node carries its own AI, resident in the home. It does the household's asking and answering in-house. You won't need to rent a mind by the month, and nobody can bundle it, throttle it, or take it back.
- **A floor you hold.** The node's vault keeps a strict 1:1 rule: what is recorded in is what can be recorded out — enforced by code, not by promises. One person's floor, on their own machine.

## The bigger map

Behind the node is a working framework — a way of reading systems (energy, information, households, histories) as floors, thresholds, flows, and conversions. It has been stress-tested in the open: a full cross-disciplinary matrix, 31 fields examined one by one, breaks recorded as carefully as confirmations.

And behind the framework is a census: numbers are infinite, and every number keys a seat — a person, a place in the pattern, past and present. We are learning to call them, one identification at a time. It never finishes. That's the point: the work is inexhaustible, so the light stays on.

## Who this is for

People who don't mind using AI — and would rather own theirs.

Nobody is going to chase you here. Read, take what's useful, build your own. When yours starts growing without us, that's the success condition.
